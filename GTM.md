# Go-To-Market — AuditAI

## Target user

Not "startups." The exact person is an **Engineering Manager or CTO at a Series A startup, 15–80 people, who owns both the engineering budget and the tooling decisions**. They are paying for 3–6 AI tools across their team, have never done a consolidated audit of that spend, and feel vaguely guilty every month when the credit card statement arrives. They are not price-sensitive in the startup sense — they will happily pay $40/seat for the right tool — but they hate paying for the *wrong* tool or the wrong *tier* of the right tool. They know Cursor exists. They know Claude exists. They do not know whether their team is on the right plan for their actual usage pattern.

Secondary target: **indie hackers and technical founders** running 2–5 person teams on a tight runway. These users have more price sensitivity and will forward the audit link to their co-founder or advisor.

## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 paid budget)

**Week 1 — Seed with high-signal communities:**
1. Post a **Show HN** on Hacker News: *"Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor for startups — finds overspend across Cursor, Claude, Copilot, etc."* Target Tuesday or Wednesday 9am ET. A good Show HN in this category can drive 300–500 visits in 24 hours.
2. Post in **Indie Hackers** with the framing: "I audited my own AI spend and found $180/month I was wasting. So I built a tool that does this for any startup in 30 seconds."
3. Drop into **Cursor Discord** and **Windsurf Discord** — users in these communities are already actively discussing plan costs and whether to switch.

**Week 2 — Warm outreach:**
4. Cold DM 20 engineering managers on X/LinkedIn with a personalised message: *"I built something that might save your team money on AI tools — no login, takes 30 seconds, here's a real output: [link to a sample audit result]."* Sharing a real audit URL is the hook — the OG preview shows a dollar amount and triggers curiosity.
5. Post in **CTO Craft Slack** and **Rands Leadership Slack** — these are niche but the quality of user is exact target.

**Week 3–4 — Earned distribution:**
6. If the Show HN gets traction, the shareable audit URL becomes the viral loop. Every audit result is a public URL with an OG preview showing the savings amount. When users forward their audit to a CFO or co-founder, that person lands on the results page and sees the "Run your own audit" link at the top.
7. Post a Twitter thread: *"I audited 10 startup AI stacks. Here's what I found..."* Use anonymised real audit data. Each tool's savings pattern becomes a tweet.

**Unfair distribution channel:**

Credex already has relationships with **companies selling unused AI credits** — these are companies that over-purchased Cursor, Claude, or ChatGPT Enterprise and need to offload them. Their finance teams are exactly the type of user who would have run an AuditAI audit. Credex can offer to share the tool with every credit seller as part of onboarding: *"Here's how your buyers will use this to validate the purchase."* This gives AuditAI distribution inside a network Credex already owns, at zero cost.
