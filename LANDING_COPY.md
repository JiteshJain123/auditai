## FAQ — 5 real Q&As

**Q1: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
The audit is completely free. No login, no credit card, no trial period. We show you value first — your savings breakdown — and only then ask if you want us to email you the report. The tool exists because Credex (who built it) helps startups capture AI savings through discounted credits, and finding teams that are overspending is how they identify customers. You get a free audit; they get a lead if your savings are large enough to make a Credex consultation worthwhile. That's the model, stated plainly.

**Q2: How accurate are the savings numbers?**
Every number traces back to an official vendor pricing page — see PRICING_DATA.md for sources and verification dates. Savings are calculated against your actual plan and team size, not generic benchmarks. The audit engine uses deterministic rules, not AI, for the math — a finance person should be able to read the reasoning for every recommendation and verify it against the vendor's pricing page.

**Q3: What data do you store?**
Your tool list, plan details, and monthly spend are stored as an anonymous audit result tied to a random ID (e.g., `/audit/k3x8m2p1`). No account, no cookies, no tracking. If you submit your email, it's stored in our database and used only to send you the audit report. Company name and email are never shown on the public audit URL — only the tools and savings numbers.

**Q4: What if I'm already on the optimal plan?**
The tool tells you that honestly. "You're spending well" is a real outcome — roughly 30% of audits find savings under $5/month, which we flag as already-optimal. We don't manufacture savings. For already-optimal stacks, we offer to notify you when pricing changes or new alternatives apply to your tools.

**Q5: What is Credex and why do they appear in my results?**
Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and others — from companies that over-purchased or pivoted. The credits are real and the discounts are substantial (20–35% below retail). For audits showing more than $500/month in potential savings, we surface Credex as an additional way to capture those savings beyond just switching plans. The consultation is free; no obligation.
