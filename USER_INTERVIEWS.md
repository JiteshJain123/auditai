# User Interviews — AuditAI

Three conversations conducted between May 8–10, 2026. Around 10–12 minutes each. All three were shown the live tool at https://auditai-alpha.vercel.app/ during the call.

---

## Interview 1 — Rahul J., working professional, private company

**Date:** May 8, 2026, ~7:30 PM
**Format:** WhatsApp video call, ~11 minutes
**Background:** Rahul works at a private firm in Indore. He uses ChatGPT free version sometimes for writing emails and checking grammar. He has never paid for any AI tool and was not sure what most of the tools on the form even were.

**Quotes:**

> "Ye kya cheez hai bhai — ye mera kuch data toh nahi le raha? Main bas samajhna chahta hoon ye karta kya hai exactly."

> "Cursor, Windsurf — ye sab kya hain? Sirf ChatGPT ko jaanta hoon main. Baaki tools ke baare mein kuch nahi pata."

> "Seat matlab kya hota hai? Mere liye toh sirf main hoon, toh seat 1 hogi na? Ye team size aur seat alag cheez hai kya?"

> "Free mein use kar raha hoon toh ye tool mujhe kuch nahi batayega na? Ye toh sirf paid walo ke liye hai."

**Most surprising thing:** He spent the first 3 minutes just worried that the tool was connecting to his accounts or collecting his data somewhere. He kept asking "ye kuch install toh nahi karna padega?" even after I explained it's just a form. The word "audit" made him nervous — he associated it with government tax audit.

**What it changed:** Added a short line at the top of the form — "No account connection needed. You enter your numbers, we do the math." Also made me realise the landing page should mention "no login, no account needed" more clearly in the hero section.

## Interview 2 — Sangeeta J., homemaker, occasional ChatGPT free user

**Date:** May 9, 2026, ~10:30 AM
**Format:** In-person, shared laptop screen, ~10 minutes
**Background:** Sangeeta uses ChatGPT free tier sometimes for recipes, letter writing, and helping kids with homework. She has never paid for any AI subscription and does not know about tools like Cursor or Claude.

**Quotes:**

> "Bhai itne saare tools hain yahan — Cursor, Claude, Windsurf. Main toh bas ChatGPT jaanti hoon. Ye sab kya hai?"

> "Ye free hai na? Kuch charge toh nahi hoga mujhe? Website pe kuch khareedna toh nahi padega?"

> "Email kyun maang raha hai ye? Main email nahi dunga kisi bhi website ko jab tak mujhe samajh na aaye ye kya karenge usse."

> "Agar main free mein use kar rahi hoon ChatGPT, toh is tool se mujhe kya milega? Kuch savings toh hongi nahi mere liye."

**Most surprising thing:** She actually tried to fill the form for ChatGPT but got confused when she saw "plan" options like Plus, Team, Enterprise. She said "ye toh itne options hain, main kaunsa choose karoon — mujhe nahi pata main kaunse plan pe hoon." She didn't know the difference between free and Plus. She selected Plus by mistake thinking free and Plus were the same thing.

**What it changed:** Added a "Free / $0" option as the very first and most visible choice in every plan dropdown so people on free tiers can immediately select it and move on. Also made the plan label say "Free (no subscription)" to make it extra clear. Before this, free users felt the form was not for them.

## Interview 3 — Bhavesh E., 3rd year B.Tech CSE, IIITM Gwalior

**Date:** May 10, 2026, ~4:00 PM
**Format:** In-person at college, ~12 minutes
**Background:** Bhavesh is a computer science student who uses ChatGPT free and GitHub Copilot free (through GitHub Student Developer Pack). He occasionally uses Cursor free tier for college projects. He has never paid for any AI tool personally.

**Quotes:**

> "Bhai students ke liye toh ye mostly kaam nahi karega — hum sab free tier pe hain. GitHub Student Pack se Copilot free mil jaata hai. Toh mera saving toh zero hi rahega."

> "Per month kitna save hoga ye batata hai — but agar main free use kar raha hoon toh saving kaise calculate hogi? Ye toh bas paid users ke liye hai."

> "Ye Credex kya hai jo results mein aa raha hai? Pehli baar sun raha hoon. Lagta hai ye tool sirf unhi ke liye bana hai — thoda ajeeb laga."

> "Agar mere college ke koi professor ya seniors jo paid tools use kar rahe hain, unhe ye send karoon toh useful rahega. Mere liye personally kuch nahi tha."

**Most surprising thing:** He pointed out that most students and freshers will land on this tool and immediately feel it's not for them because all their tools are free. He said "ye tool ka target user clearly koi aur hai — working professional ya startup wala. Student aaya toh kuch nahi milega usse." That was honest and made me think the landing page should say upfront who this is actually for.

**What it changed:** Added a note on the landing page and form that says "Best for teams spending $50+/month on AI tools." This sets the right expectation so students or free-tier users are not confused about why their savings come out as zero.
